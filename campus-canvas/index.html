Why Campus Canvas exists

Most university art and memorabilia is chosen by institutions, marketers or vendors. Campus Canvas reverses that process. Students create the source material and students help validate what feels authentic.

The result is a continuously improving library of visual memories that ArtUP applies to publish posters, prints, limited editions and, ultimately, premium artist-curated originals for graduation and alumni gifting.

Simple by design

The voting experience is simple. Students move through selected images and indicate whether each one resonates with them. ArtUP uses the combined preference data to understand which subjects have the strongest connection across the student community.

From a phone pic to fine curated art

A photograph does not need to be technically perfect to matter. It needs to capture something real. Campus Canvas helps ArtUP identify those real moments at scale. The strongest images can then move from student memory to artist interpretation — preserving the emotional connection while creating something new.
