Campus Canvas

You lived it. You help shape it.

Campus Canvas is where Queen’s students share memories, explore submitted images, and help ArtUp understand which parts of university life feel most meaningful. Student choices guide the themes artists will see—they do not judge artistic quality.

Open Campus Canvas (opens in a new tab)

On a computer? Scan with your phone’s camera to open Campus Canvas there.

Inside Campus Canvas

Three simple ways to take part.

  1. Share a memory

    Submit a photograph and a few words about a place, person, or moment that shaped your time at Queen’s.

  2. Explore

    Browse the memories other Queen’s students have shared.

  3. Show what resonates

    Mark the memories that feel meaningful to you. There is no wrong answer.

Student choices guide the themes artists see. They don’t decide whether an artist—or an image—is good.

Your memories, handled with care.

  • Every submission is reviewed by the ArtUp team before it appears in Campus Canvas.
  • You keep the copyright to the photographs you share.
  • Campus Canvas shows its terms — including how ArtUp may use submitted photographs and stories — before you submit anything.
