About ArtUP Life

About Us

ArtUP Life transforms the memories that define the university experience into art that Inspires a lifestyle of continued success. We invite students to share and help select the places, moments and experiences that matter most, then work with artists to interpret those memories as distinctive original art pieces. The result is more than a graduation gift—it's an heirloom of the university experience, created to preserve where you've been and inspire where you're going.
