How it works

A memory becomes something lasting.

  1. Share a Memory

    Students submit photographs and stories about the places, people, and moments that shaped their Queen’s experience.

  2. Explore Campus Canvas

    Students browse submissions and indicate which memories resonate. There is no wrong answer.

  3. Artists Interpret

    Kingston artists transform student-validated themes through their own styles and creative voices.

  4. The Collection Arrives

    Families discover a limited graduation collection rooted in experiences their graduate actually lived.

Campus Canvas

You lived it. You help shape it.

Campus Canvas is where Queen’s students share memories, explore submitted images, and help ArtUp understand which parts of university life feel most meaningful. Student choices guide the themes artists will see—they do not judge artistic quality.

  • Every submission is reviewed by the ArtUp team before it appears in Campus Canvas.
  • You keep the copyright to the photographs you share.
  • Campus Canvas shows its terms — including how ArtUp may use submitted photographs and stories — before you submit anything.

About ArtUp

Some achievements deserve a reminder you can live with.

Graduation marks years of growth, friendship, challenge, and possibility. ArtUp exists to preserve that experience through original local art—not as a generic campus souvenir, but as a meaningful work connected to memories students recognize as their own.

Queen’s University and Kingston are the first chapter of a model designed to honour what makes every university community distinct.
