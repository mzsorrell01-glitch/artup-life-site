How the contest works

Submit exactly three different images. You may make an unlimited number of separate three-image submissions during the submission period, but the same image may not be submitted twice. (Section 5)

Images are reviewed before they appear. ArtUp accepts or rejects each image, and images must not show a recognizable individual. (Sections 5 and 6)

Eligible Campus Canvas users vote. Each user may cast one recorded vote per image, which may be positive or negative. Voting on your own images is permitted. (Section 9)

Rankings use total positive votes. Negative votes do not subtract from an image’s total. The five eligible images with the highest positive-vote counts win, subject to review and tie-breaking. (Sections 9 and 11)

Voting closes December 11, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time. ArtUp expects to send potential-winner notices within 10 business days. (Sections 3 and 11)

11Winner Selection and Tie-Breaking

After voting closes, ArtUp will review the results and determine the five eligible images with the highest total positive-vote counts, after removing ineligible images, invalidating improper votes, applying eligibility requirements, and resolving ties.

If two or more eligible images have the same positive-vote total for a prize position, ArtUp staff will break the tie using criteria such as relevance to the Queen's University experience, originality, visual quality, suitability for artistic reinterpretation, and commercial usability. ArtUp's tie-breaking determination is final and binding, subject to applicable law.

If more than one image from the same entrant would otherwise receive a prize, ArtUp may, in its discretion, limit that entrant to one prize. If ArtUp elects to apply a one-prize-per-entrant limit, it will apply that approach consistently when finalizing winners, and the next-highest-ranked eligible image(s) will move into the available prize position(s).

ArtUp expects to complete result review and send potential-winner notices within 10 business days after the voting deadline, subject to any lawful extension, investigation, verification, or administrative delay reasonably required to protect contest integrity.
