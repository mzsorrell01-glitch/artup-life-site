How It Works

ArtUP starts with students, not with a marketing team. The collection is shaped by the visual memories students choose to submit and validate together. That creates a direct line from lived experience to the art.

1

Students submit the memories that matter

Students upload original photographs of the places, details and experiences that capture their time at University. A library corner, a limestone detail, the waterfront, a familiar street, a campus ritual or any image that holds real meaning.

2

The student community validate the images

Submissions move into the Campus Canvas enabling students to review and indicate which ones resonate with them. The purpose is simple: identify the visual memories that feel most authentical of their University experience.

3

Artists interpret — they do not simply reproduce

Selected images become creative source material. Participating artists interpret the subject in their own style — contemporary, abstract, impressionistic, street-influenced, figurative or mixed media — creating art with both a connection to place and an independent artistic identity.

4

The collection is presented to graduates and families

Finished works are catalogued and presented through ArtUP Life. Graduating students and Alumni can re-discover the story behind the image and select a piece that ignites feelings and inspires for a life of continued success.

5

The memory becomes part of the life ahead

The result is more than a picture of campus. They are student-validated memories transformed into art — and, for selected original fine-art works, an heirloom of the university experience that can remain meaningful for decades.
