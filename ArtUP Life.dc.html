The Queen's Graduate Collection

A limited collection shaped by the students who lived it.

Each year, Queen's students help identify the campus views, Kingston streets, University interiors, traditions and quiet details that best represent their experience. Local artists then interpret those student-selected images in their own distinctive style, creating original works with an authentic connection to place.

About ArtUP Life

We believe Art elevates Llife.

ArtUP Life was created around a simple belief: the things we choose to live with, influence how we see ourselves, how we experience our surroundings, and how we are inspired to achieve what comes next.

We are drawn to art because it does more than decorate a wall. The right piece can hold memory. It can create atmosphere. It can mark achievement. It can become part of a family story. And over time, it can become inseparable from the place we call home.

Our first program brings that philosophy to graduating university students. We begin at Queen's University in Kingston, Ontario, where students themselves help identify the places and images that best represent their university experience.

The result is deliberately hyper-local and authentic: art shaped by students, created by local artists and chosen by families as a meaningful way to celebrate one of life's most important transitions.

From the 2027 Kingston pilot

For parents

For parents, it is an opportunity to give something enduring - a gift connected to the years their child has just completed, but sophisticated enough to belong in the life they are beginning.

For graduates

For graduates, it is an invitation to start living with art: to surround themselves with objects that have beauty, craftsmanship, meaning and emotional resonance.

That is the idea behind ArtUP Life. Not simply to own art - but to make art part of the life you are creating.
